Welcome! This is the first post on my new site. ✨

## Why a blog?

Writing things down is the best way I know to really understand them. If I can explain an idea clearly, I've actually learned it — and if it helps someone else along the way, even better.

## What to expect

I'll be writing about the topics you see on the home page:

- **AI & Agents** — how LLM-powered agents work, tools, patterns and experiments.
- **Software Engineering** — things I build and lessons learned.
- **Ideas & Curiosities** — anything else that catches my interest.

> Learning in public: share what you learn, as you learn it.

Thanks for stopping by — see you in the next post!
